Rationale and priorities
Why the redesign looks the way it does, and what it costs to build.

Key decisions

1
Gradient header becomes a collapsing large top barThe old header spent about 20% of the screen on decoration. The title now shrinks on scroll and the version string moves to Settings, so lists start higher and the brand stays visible.
2
One accent, eight chain colors with a single jobViolet is the only interface accent. Chain colors appear only on chain badges and chips, so color means “which cinema” and nothing else. The old brown and orange buttons are gone.
3
Chains become summary cardsEach card shows how many venues are selected and expands in place. You can see the whole selection without opening anything, instead of eight identical “Add venue” pills.
4
Sticky action bar, one primary action per screenSearch and Clear stay reachable at any scroll depth. Copy poll is a floating action on Results, because it is the reason people open that screen.
5
Poster first, showtimes in aligned columnsA 104×156dp poster leads each card. Rows follow a fixed order (time, chain, format, ticket), the date appears once as a heading, and the ticket is a clear icon button with a real hit area.
6
Tonal depth instead of outlinesFour surface steps separate layers. Shadows are used only on floating elements, and there is no blur, which keeps Android scrolling cheap.
7
State never depends on color aloneSelected chips carry a check, selected days a fill and a weight change, today a ring. Text pairs are 4.5:1 or better; the exceptions are listed below.

Build priority

Cheap

Pure theming and layout, no new architecture

  • CinemerickTheme object, colors, shapes, spacing, Plus Jakarta Sans scale
  • Unified buttons, chips, format tags, day tiles
  • Showtime row restyle and the per-day heading
  • Film card layout with the larger poster
  • Sticky bottom bar and the Copy poll FAB
  • Settings: segmented controls, switch, RangeSlider
  • chainEdge stroke in dark theme
  • Outline icon set replacing the emoji glyphs

Medium

New structure, still standard Compose

  • Collapsing large top bar with scroll behavior
  • Expandable chain cards (AnimatedVisibility, state hoisted per chain)
  • Film sheet: backdrop scrim, LazyRow carousels, Read more
  • Venue picker as a searchable multi-select sheet
  • Adaptive layouts from window size class: two-pane tablet, inline calendar on desktop
  • Navigation rail on desktop

Ambitious

Motion and platform polish

  • Shared-element poster transition into the details sheet; test on iOS before committing
  • Staggered list entrance with the animations toggle wired through
  • Hover, focus rings and keyboard navigation on desktop
  • Image placeholders and crossfade for posters and backdrops
  • Three-column poster grid above 1600dp

Open points

  • The Space orange with white text is 3.33:1. The brand pair is kept as requested. It passes for bold labels at 14sp and above, not for small regular text. If you want strict AA, switch only that chip’s text to the dark 1A1A1A (about 5.2:1) and leave the fill alone.
  • Cinemazero, Cinergia and UCI nearly disappear on the dark ground (1.1, 2.1 and 1.8:1 against the card). The chainEdge stroke fixes the shape; it is the only addition to the brand colors.
  • Sample content. Film titles, venue names, ratings and the version are placeholders; posters and photos are gradients.
  • Italian strings are not drawn yet. Rows and chips are built to wrap rather than clip, so a pass with real labels should be quick.
